Hike Spa Dinner

Bukhansan Hike + Dragon Hill Spa Recovery

Morning: Bukhansan National Park — the Baegundae Peak trail (836m, ~3–4hr round trip), the classic route with sweeping views over Seoul and peak fall foliage in October. Getting there: from Hannam-dong, take the subway to Gupabal Station (Line 3, ~30 min with one transfer), then bus 704 to the Bukhansanseong trailhead (~20 min). Total door-to-trailhead is about an hour. Wear real hiking shoes — the upper section involves rope-assisted rock scrambling.

Afternoon: head straight to Dragon Hill Spa — Seoul's most famous jjimjilbang, rooftop garden, multiple themed saunas, traditional Korean body scrub (seshin). Perfect timing for sore legs. Book the full package. Evening: Gangnam for dinner.

Afternoon/evening: Spa Land at Shinsegae Centum City — 18 mineral hot spring pools, 13 themed saunas, seshin body scrub. The best spa in Korea by any measure. Budget half a day here.

Tongyeong Day Trip + Jagalchi Market

Day trip to Tongyeong — a coastal town about 1.5–2hrs from Busan. Getting there: intercity bus from Busan Seobu (Sasang) Bus Terminal to Tongyeong Bus Terminal (~1h40min, departures every 20–30 min, no reservation needed). Evening: back in Busan, head to Jagalchi Fish Market — pick live fish from the stalls, they cook it upstairs on the spot. Busan's version of a food adventure.

Korea's largest seafood market. Walk past stalls of live octopus, abalone, and fresh catch. Point at what you want — the restaurant upstairs grills or serves it raw (hoe). Very Stanley-shrimp-allergy-navigable.

🛍

Seongsu-dong Shopping

Seoul's coolest neighborhood in 2024/2025. MUSINSA Megastore, ADERERROR, Matin Kim. Bespoke leather shoes made to order on Shoe Street. More pop-up stores per block than anywhere else in Korea.

🏯

Haedong Yonggungsa Temple

Buddhist temple built directly onto Busan's sea cliffs. One of the few coastal temples in Korea — dramatic autumn light, crashing waves below. Zero crowds compared to the Gyeongbokgung palaces in Seoul.

Seoul Shopping

Brands worth the detour.

Two neighborhoods carry the best of Seoul fashion. Seongsu-dong is the flagship-and-pop-up hub — Time Out's 4th coolest neighborhood globally. Itaewon/Hannam is where the more editorial, designer-forward labels live.

Seongsu-dong

  • MUSINSA Just opened a 6,600 sqm megastore (April 2026). Korea's dominant K-fashion platform, now IRL.
  • ADERERROR Seoul's favorite experimental streetwear brand. Sculptural, wearable, unmistakably Korean.
  • Matin Kim — Seongsu Store The Seongsu flagship has the full range — clean, elevated Korean womenswear with a subtle sculptural quality. Better selection than most stockists abroad.
  • Covernat Workwear-inspired Korean streetwear. Less hype, more substance.
  • OSOI Minimalist Korean leather goods and bags — clean shapes, soft leather, quietly popular.
  • Gentle Monster / NUDAKE Eyewear flagship + their café concept. Best matcha croissant in Seoul.
  • Seongsu Shoe Street Bespoke leather shoes made by craftsmen who've been here since the 1970s. ₩150–300k (~$110–220).

Itaewon / Hannam-dong

  • KIJUN "Standard" in Korean — womenswear that redefines it. Cinematic collections inspired by '80s and '90s Seoul street style. Structured coats, flared pants, bubble skirts. Store at 2F, 19 Itaewon-ro 54-gil. Open 12–8pm daily.
  • Gentle Monster Flagship The original Hongdae flagship — avant-garde eyewear in an installation-art setting. Worth visiting even if you don't buy anything.

Before You Go

Logistics.

📱

Download these apps

  • Naver Maps — primary navigation. The only Korean map app with full English support. Better than Google Maps for turn-by-turn, subway exits, and restaurant reviews (receipt-verified).
  • Kakao T — taxis. Korea's Uber. Book ahead, skip the curb wait, pay digitally. Show the driver your destination on Naver Maps if there's a language barrier.
  • T-money — subway and bus payments. Pick up a physical T-money card at the airport (7-Eleven or GS25 inside arrivals), or use the app. Tap on/off at every gate.
🚄

Seoul → Busan by KTX

  • Depart from Seoul Station (서울역) — Lines 1 and 4, also the AREX airport express terminus. Most convenient KTX hub in the city.
  • Journey time: 2h 15min · 20+ trains daily · ~$40/person economy
  • Book in advance at Korail or Rail.Ninja — October is busy and window seats go fast.
  • Alternative: Suseo Station (SRT) is slightly cheaper but less central. Stick with Seoul Station KTX.
✈️

Airport → Hotel

  • AREX Airport Express from ICN Terminal 1 directly to Seoul Station — ~43 min, ~$9. Fastest option.
  • KAL Limousine Bus stops at major hotel areas including Hongdae, Yongsan/Itaewon, and Gangnam — ~60–90 min depending on traffic, ~$18.
  • Kakao T taxi from airport runs ~$50–70 and 60–90 min. Fine if you have luggage and multiple people splitting.
💳

Money and payments

  • Credit cards accepted almost everywhere in Seoul and Busan. Visa/Mastercard fine; Amex less reliable at smaller spots.
  • Carry some cash (₩) for street food, markets, and smaller cafés. ATMs at every convenience store (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven).
  • Tax refund on shopping: eligible if you spend ₩30,000+ at participating stores. Get a refund receipt and claim at ICN airport before departure.
