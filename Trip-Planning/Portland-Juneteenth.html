Why Portland, Why Now

The PNW in late June — roads are dry, Mt. Hood is in view, and the city is fully alive. Four days of big rides through the hills, food cart lunches mid-route, Bike Play on Friday night, and a proper group dinner at Eem Thai on Saturday. This is what a trip with bikes is supposed to feel like.


The Airbnb is in NE Portland — walkable to Alberta Arts, Mississippi, and Williams. Two minivans for easy transport. Three big rides mapped and ready, all starting and ending at the house. Here's how it goes.

Eat Well

Portland's food scene rewards curiosity.

The city's food cart culture means great meals can happen mid-ride. These are the sit-down spots worth planning around, plus the essentials you shouldn't miss.

Must-Do
  • Pine State Biscuits Southern Biscuits · Alberta Friday breakfast. 7-minute ride from the Airbnb. Biscuit sandwiches, sausage gravy, honey butter. Order the Reggie.
  • Eem Thai Thai BBQ · Saturday 6 PM The Saturday night reservation. 10 people, booked under Sophia on OpenTable. 24-hour cancellation, $50/pp. 15-min walk from Airbnb.
  • Ant Farm Grilled Cheese · Sandy · Mile 37 Mid-ride stop on Thursday's Ant Farm ride. Grilled cheese in Sandy at roughly the halfway point. The reason the route has a name.
  • Sellwood Food Cart Pod Food Carts · Mile 64 Second stop on Thursday's ride. Multiple options, eat what looks good, fuel up for the final stretch home.
Worth Adding
  • Jeju Korean · NE Portland Korean restaurant in NE. Good option for a group dinner if you want something different from Thai on one of the earlier nights.
  • Urdaneta Basque Tapas Pintxos and Basque small plates. Great for a group — order a lot of things, share everything. Good wine list.
  • Rangoon Bistro Burmese · Mississippi Ave 4.9 stars, 370+ reviews. The giant pork-and-cabbage dumpling in chili oil is the move. Open for lunch Fri–Sun.
  • ¿Por Qué No? Tacos · Mississippi Ave Classic Portland taco spot. Great patio, al pastor tacos, fun vibe for 8 people on a sunny afternoon.
Coffee
  • Bastion Coffee Specialty Coffee A Portland roaster worth going out of your way for. Good for a pre-ride morning stop.
  • Portland Cà Phê Vietnamese Coffee Exclusively Vietnamese coffee: house-made ube syrup lattes, cheese foam drinks, cà phê muối (salted coffee). Distinctly Portland.
  • Paadee Thai Café More casual Thai option in the neighborhood. Good for lunch or an early dinner when you don't need a reservation.
  • S'mores Bar Dessert Because obviously. Good group stop after dinner on a walk through the neighborhood.
