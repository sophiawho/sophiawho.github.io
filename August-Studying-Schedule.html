Rules of engagement

  • Finish all MUST DOs in P1 before starting any P2 follow-up. That's the whole point of dropping dates — breadth across the topics that are actually being asked beats depth in one.

  • Falling behind? Cut in this order: P3 follow-ups → P3 must-dos → P2 follow-ups. Never cut a P1 must-do or the mock debrief.
