How this block works — read first

  • The loop is DS/A-heavy. Rounds 1 and 3 are DS/A (45 min each), round 2 is systems design (60 min). That's the reverse of the Plaid/Retool/Scale loops you just finished, so the weighting here flips accordingly — 19 problems against 12 hours of design.
  • Topic order is deliberate. Backtracking first (Aug 11–12) because it's your oldest flagged gap and the choose/explore/unchoose skeleton is a prerequisite for palindrome partitioning and N-Queens. DP lands on the weekend (Aug 16–17) where there's room to actually build the recurrence rather than pattern-match it. Trees and intervals sit last because they're your strongest — they're a confidence taper, not new learning.
  • Systems design = 12h total, split 1 + 1.5 + 1 + 3.5 + 3.5 + 1.5. The two big weekend blocks are unallocated on purpose — TODO · fill in sys design focus. Everything else is scheduled around them.
  • Aug 19 is deliberately light and Aug 20 has no new material. Cramming a new pattern the night before an onsite has never once helped.
  • Log every problem in learning_journal.html the same day. Retry dates matter more than volume here.
